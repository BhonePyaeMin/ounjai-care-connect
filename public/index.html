How OunJai works

  1. Create an account

    Register with basic information and accept the privacy notice.

  2. Search for services

    Childcare, elderly care, disability support or rehabilitation.

  3. Find a suitable caregiver

    Compare AI match scores, qualifications, availability and fees.

  4. Book a service

    Choose the date, time and place and submit your request.

  5. Receive care and track

    Follow the caregiver's arrival and use SOS if needed.

  6. Give feedback

    Rate the service to help others and improve quality.
